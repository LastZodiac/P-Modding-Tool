P+ Blender Kit (test)
=====================

Separate from P+ Modding Tool. Nothing here changes your SD card: the costume file it makes is added
with P+ Modding Tool's "Add from file...", through the usual safety checks.
You need Blender 4 (or newer) installed. Nothing has to be installed into Blender.


THE WHOLE THING, START TO FINISH
  1. Open "P+ Blender Bridge.exe".
  2. Pick the fighter and the costume (for example Peach, costume 11) > "Open it in Blender".
     (The first time, if your Blender isn't found, it asks where blender.exe is. Only once.)
  3. Blender opens with the costume loaded and the "P+ Kit" panel open on the right.
     The panel shows 6 steps and only what to do on the step you're on:
       1  Open the costume         (already done by the Bridge)
       2  Your model               "Open your model" > pick it (GLB, glTF, FBX or OBJ)
       3  Clean it up              Turn it, make it bigger/smaller, take off the hair and other pieces
       4  Line up the joints       "Show the joint dots", move any dot that's off, "Fit my model to P+"
       5  Shape it and finish      Fix small things with the shape handles, then "Use P+'s head"
                                   or "Keep my model's head"
       6  Check and save           "Play a test move", then "Save and make the costume file"
  4. The Bridge pops up by itself and makes the costume file, with before/after pictures
     (standing, waiting, side smash). A folder opens with the costume file in it.
  5. P+ Modding Tool > "Add from file..." > that costume file.
  Made a mistake anywhere? Ctrl+Z steps back, as many times as you need.


STEP 3: CLEANING UP YOUR MODEL
  - It comes in at the costume's size (matched by where the hands are, so long hair or a crown
    don't throw it off). Still off? "Smaller" / "Bigger".
  - "Take off the hair (for P+'s head)": takes off the hair and other loose pieces on the head
    (earrings, hats) in one click, when they're separate pieces of the model.
  - Anything left: "Click pieces" > click it (it turns orange) > "Take off the whole piece".
    For a part that's joined to the body: "Box a part" > drag a box around it >
    "Take off just what's selected".
  - "Put it all back" brings back your model as it came in.


STEP 4: LINING UP THE JOINTS
  - The dots sit on P+'s joints (hips, neck, shoulders, elbows, wrists, knees, ankles, toes).
  - Click a dot, then move it onto the same joint of your model with the arrow buttons
    (or G and the mouse). "Both sides at once" moves the other side's dot too.
  - "Front view" / "Side view" to check both ways. Then "Fit my model to P+".


STEP 5: SHAPE HANDLES (small fixes without remodelling)
  "Show the shape handles" puts a handle on each part (hips, body, head, upper arms, forearms,
  hands, thighs, shins, feet). Click one, then:
    Up / Down / Left / Right / Toward front / Toward back   nudge it
    Longer / Shorter                                        stretch it (the rest of the arm or leg follows)
    Thicker / Thinner                                       make it wider or slimmer
    Turn                                                    turn it a little
    Reset this one / Reset all                              undo the changes
  "Both sides at once" does the same to the other side. "Keep this shape" when you're happy.
  Examples: arms a bit short > click a forearm > Longer. A foot pointing out > click it > Turn.


GOOD TO KNOW
  - Where's the costume file? Documents > P+ Blender Kit > (the costume) > "(the costume) (from Blender)",
    for example Documents\P+ Blender Kit\Peach costume 11\Peach costume 11 (from Blender)\FitPeach19.pac.
    The Bridge's "Show the costume file" button opens that folder with the file picked out.
  - Your model's picture is redone when you finish (one tidy picture for what's left of it, without the
    hair that was taken off), so it stays sharp when it's made smaller to fit the game's memory.
  - "Use P+'s head" keeps the costume's own head, hair, eyes and blinking exactly as they were.
    It takes a lot of the fighter's memory, so your model's pictures may be made smaller to fit
    (the Bridge says so). "Keep my model's head" leaves more room for sharper pictures.
  - Always save with the panel's "Save and make the costume file". A file saved with Blender's own
    Export has no P+ skeleton (or has the costume in it twice), and the Bridge says so.
  - If the Bridge didn't pop up after saving: open it, pick the fighter and costume, use
    "Choose what you saved from Blender..." and pick the "... - from Blender.glb" file.
  - "Install the P+ Kit into Blender anyway (optional)" puts the P+ Kit tab in Blender for good
    (press N over the 3D view to see it), for opening Blender on your own. You don't need it
    when you use "Open it in Blender".
  - Things the fighter only shows sometimes (Peach's racket, pan and club, other faces) aren't
    sent to Blender; they're always put back from the costume.
