P+ Modding Tool, by Last Zodiac
===============================
Everything for Project+ on Brawl Online in one app:
  - Mods: costumes (skins), stages, music and texture packs, from a file or straight from the Mod Store
    (GameBanana). Every stage is checked so you stay in sync online. Turn mods on and off any time.
  - Your own music: MP3 or WAV files made into songs the game plays, with your own loop point and volume.
  - Model Swapper (beta): put a 3D model from another game or program on a P+ character.
  - Stat Tracker: your form and Smash stats (openings per kill, neutral wins, kill moves...), each character
    you play, a simple match list with every game's details, records by matchup, stage and opponent, all from
    Brawl Online's own game history and replays. Plus an in-game overlay with your opponent's info.
  - Frame Data: every fighter's moves with their hitboxes frame by frame, projectiles, launch angles,
    true combos, and how to DI each hit (drag yourself onto the move), read straight from P+'s own files.
    Save any move as a GIF.

Needs: Windows 10 or 11 and Brawl Online installed on this PC.

Your mods, downloads and the backups of P+'s own files are kept in the "Data" folder next to the app.
The app looks for new versions by itself; you can also check in Settings.

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC" when you first open Setup: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
(The app isn't signed with a paid certificate, so Windows doesn't know it yet.)
